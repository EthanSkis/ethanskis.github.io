Detail Valley

A spotless car, without leaving your driveway.

Detail Valley is my mobile car detailing service for Valley County, Idaho. I designed the brand, built the site, and wrote the booking flow, and I'm also the one who shows up with the pressure washer. Around here a spotless car usually costs you a Saturday and a drive to a shop. The whole idea of Detail Valley is that it shouldn't.

01

The shop drives to you

Everything rides along: water-fed gear, pro-grade products, towels, the lot. All a job needs on site is an outdoor tap and a power outlet. That flips the usual geometry of detailing, so instead of every customer driving to one shop, one van reaches every driveway from McCall through Donnelly to Cascade.

One van, one highway, every driveway in reach. Open Monday through Saturday, with same-week slots.

02

Booked in a minute

No phone tag, no account, no deposit. You pick a package, a day, and an arrival window, and that's the whole transaction until the work is done. Payment happens after the walkaround, when you've checked every panel and seat: cash, check, card, Venmo, PayPal, or Cash App. If you're not happy, I fix it.

03

Flat pricing, four packages

Every job is one of four packages with a flat price, so there are no surprises on the day. The Maintenance Wash is a proper two-bucket hand wash at $85. The Interior Detail covers shampoo, surfaces, vents, and glass inside for $185. The Full Detail combines both at $295, and Showroom adds clay bar decontamination and wax for $360. Showroom is a clay-and-wax finish, not paint correction, and the site says so plainly.

Wash, Full, and Showroom each contain the tier before it; the dashed line marks where the previous package ends. Interior Detail, dashed, stands alone.

04

Proof in the driveway

The site leans on evidence instead of adjectives: twenty before-and-after pairs, each photographed on the same vehicle on the same day, from mud-caked 4Runner floor mats back to bare rubber, to pine needles vacuumed out of a Crosstrek's rear footwells. Under those sit real five-star reviews pulled from the Google Business profile.

The gallery on the site works exactly like this: drag the handle, same vehicle, same day.

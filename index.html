Questions
What if you stop doing this someday?

You're covered. Your domain is registered in your name, and I hand over everything it takes to run the site. Any web developer can pick it up from there. You're never locked in to me.

How long does it take?

Your free mockup takes a few days. Once you approve it, most sites are live in 1 to 2 weeks. Custom work with booking or ordering takes longer, and I'll give you a date up front.

Do I have to write everything?

No. We talk for 15 minutes about your business, I write a first draft, and you fix anything that's off. Send me photos if you have them. If not, I can take some.

What's a Google Business Profile, and why does it matter?

It's the box that shows up on Google and Google Maps with your hours, phone number, photos and reviews. For a local business it often brings in more calls than the website itself. Every package includes setting it up or cleaning up the one you have, and linking it to your new site.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Most clients would rather text me, and that's what the Care Plan is for. If you'd rather make changes yourself, I can build your site with a simple editor where you log in and change text, prices, photos and PDFs without touching code. That's part of a Custom build.

Do I have to get the Care Plan?

No. Without it, I hand you the finished site and you host it wherever you like. The Care Plan is for people who'd rather text me "we're closed Monday" and have it handled.
